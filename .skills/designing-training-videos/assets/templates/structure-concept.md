# Structure: Concept / literacy (Jumbo default)

**Canonical timing, wording rules, and worked example:**
[`courses/notes/jumbo-ai-video-structure-guide.md`](../../../../courses/notes/jumbo-ai-video-structure-guide.md)

Skill pointer:
[references/jumbo-video-structure.md](../../references/jumbo-video-structure.md)

Use when the goal is a **shared mental model** - one idea, plain language
first, term second. This is the **default shape for AI Foundations** videos.

## Shape (six-minute default)

| # | Beat | Time | Words | Job |
|---|------|------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:30 | ~70 | Meeting moment. No agenda, no definitions. Implicit promise. |
| 2 | Concept | 0:30–2:30 | ~280 | Plain language. **Zero new terms.** Analogy + where it breaks. |
| 3 | Name it | 2:30–3:30 | ~140 | Attach the term. `[PAUSE]`. On screen. Define as callback. |
| 4 | In the wild | 3:30–4:30 | ~140 | Real colleague sentence. Unpack. Flag common misuse. |
| 5 | So what | 4:30–5:30 | ~140 | Judgement, trap, question, or risk - specific behaviour. |
| 6 | Recap | 5:30–6:00 | ~70 | Three lines: concept / term / behaviour. Spoken + on screen. Stop. |

Scale with runtime (3 / 5 / 6 / 7 min) per the guide. Cap at 8 minutes.
Below 3 minutes: Hook → Concept → Name it → Recap only.

## Beat writing tips

- **Hook:** name the discomfort honestly; never "in this video we will cover".
- **Concept:** if you need the term to explain the thing, the explanation is not plain enough yet.
- **Name it:** say the term, pause, put it on screen; acronym + expansion once each.
- **In the wild:** do not cut this beat for time - it is the highest-value beat for meeting literacy.
- **So what:** not "now you understand X" - a concrete change in what they notice or do.
- **Recap:** exactly three lines; no sign-off ritual.

## On-screen text pattern

- Terms and labels only during Name it / In the wild
- Recap lines (spoken + on screen is correct here)
- Never put the full narration on screen (captions are a separate track)

## Good fit examples

- 1.3 How a language model actually works
- 2.1 Tokens, context windows, and why it forgot
- 2.2 The hallucination problem
- 2.3 RAG

## Failures to avoid

- Two ideas in one video (takeaway needs an "and")
- Defining before explaining (term appears in beat 2)
- Cutting "in the wild" for time
- Unqualified analogy (always state where it breaks)
- Ending with "see you in the next video"
