# Structure: How-to / workflow

Use when the learner must **perform a task** or follow a process after
watching. One workflow per video.

## Shape

| # | Beat | Job | ~Share |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Hook | When you need this / cost of skipping it | 5–10% |
| 2 | Outcome | What "done" looks like | 5–10% |
| 3 | Steps | Demonstrate the path (3–7 steps max) | 50–60% |
| 4 | Critical decisions | Mistakes, branches, checks | 15–20% |
| 5 | Recap checklist | Ordered reminders + reinforcement | 10% |

## Beat writing tips

- Name steps with verbs the learner will do ("Draft", "Review", "Escalate").
- Show the workflow visually; narration supports, does not replace, the demo.
- Put the highest-risk mistake in its own beat with a clear signal.
- End as soon as the task is complete - no bonus theory appendix.

## On-screen text pattern

- Step n of m
- Short verb label
- Optional warning chip on the risky step

## Good fit examples

- "Draft then verify an AI-assisted email"
- "How to escalate a sensitive data question"
- "Using the approved AI tool for meeting notes"

## Failures to avoid

- Teaching three tools in one how-to
- Skipping the "done" state
- Narrating UI chrome the viewer does not need
