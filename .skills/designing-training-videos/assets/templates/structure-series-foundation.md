# Structure: Series foundation (rewatch episode)

Use for **course openers and glossary episodes** (typically Episodes 1–2).
These are reference assets: slower, clearer, more quotable.

## Shape

| # | Beat | Job | ~Share |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Series promise | What this course will make possible | 5–10% |
| 2 | Hook | Why foundations first | 5–10% |
| 3 | Core teach | Must-land at unhurried pace | 50–55% |
| 4 | Language lock | Terms they will hear in later episodes | 15–20% |
| 5 | Bridge | What comes next + how to use this as reference | 10% |
| 6 | Recap + prompt | Especially crisp must-land | 10% |

## Beat writing tips

- Optimise for **rewatch**: cards and phrases people can quote in meetings.
- Prefer fewer ideas taught well over a tour of the whole course.
- Explicitly tell viewers this episode is worth bookmarking.
- Keep wit gentle; credibility matters most here.

## On-screen text pattern

- Chapter / episode pill
- Term cards with lasting definitions
- "Save this" or "Reference" cue only if it fits brand tone

## Good fit examples

- AI Literacy Episode 1: What is AI, actually?
- AI Literacy Episode 2: The lingo decoder

## Failures to avoid

- Turning Ep 1 into a trailer for every later topic
- Racing definitions to "get to the fun stuff"
- Skipping reinforcement because "it's just intro"
